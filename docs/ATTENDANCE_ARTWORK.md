## Final prompts

### Call Off / Sick

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: polished small UI illustration for an unbranded Employee Services attendance request app. Primary request: a real bespoke illustration to replace a sleeping emoji. A young adult employee resting sick in bed with a soft coral blanket, pale pillow, a hand resting on forehead, subtle three warm coral squiggles above the forehead. Detailed professional 3D editorial illustration, soft realistic materials, expressive natural human face, polished clay-like 3D rendering. Three-quarter view, isolated compact horizontal composition, entire bed and person visible with generous transparent padding. Palette: navy pajamas, dusty coral and rose bedding, warm skin, soft studio light. Transparent background, no rectangular background, no floor plane, no typography, no logos, no watermark. Readable at 140px wide.

### Coming Late

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: polished small UI illustration for an unbranded Employee Services attendance app. Primary request: a real bespoke illustration to replace a running emoji. A young adult office employee hurrying to work, in a natural running stride, carrying a dark navy work satchel, blue shirt, navy trousers, warm skin, expressive face. A large luminous amber analog clock behind and beside the runner, clock face without numbers, to convey coming late. Detailed professional 3D editorial illustration, soft realistic materials, polished clay-like 3D rendering. Three-quarter view, isolated compact composition, entire figure and clock visible with generous transparent padding. Palette: deep blue and golden amber. Transparent background, no rectangular background, no floor plane, no typography, no logos, no watermark. Readable at 140px wide.
