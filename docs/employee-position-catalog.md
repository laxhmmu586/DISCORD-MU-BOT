Employee positions use the title catalog in `has/public/employee-positions.js` for both Admin and Request workflow validation. The source is the supplied Organizational Chart 2026.xlsx: HQ 2026 and all 2026 station sheets (LAX, SFO, HNL, OGG/KOA/LIH, SAN, ONT, CBS/L&F/CC, DFW, AUS, IAH, EWR, ATL and JFK). Historical 2023/2024 sheets are excluded.

Only position titles are carried over. Names, station assignments, employee records, order, account links, and current approval workflows are not imported or automatically reassigned. Repeated titles are deduplicated; Senior/SR/SR SUP and spelling variants are written consistently. CSA is Agent, per the user's instruction. Existing Manager and Lead remain available so existing employee records and workflow snapshots keep working.

Administrators can select the added titles for employees and workflow stages. Workflow changes apply to new requests; existing requests retain their stages. Manager may be followed by higher stages; the final configured node completes the request. Up to 20 stages are supported. Other titles do not automatically inherit approval authority: an employee must match the configured stage, and the same employee cannot approve twice. Read & Sign publishing permissions are unchanged.

The October 19 VISA syntax is already implemented in PR #1528: CHN GREEN CARD is Chinese residence, VS TO CAMBODIA binds the dated visa to Cambodia/KTI, and unqualified V EXP can use a checked Vietnam outbound route. The regression tests preserve destination, expiry and negative-evidence checks. No passenger names or passport numbers are included in new fixtures.

Approval Workflow now opens `/approval-workflow.html` as a dedicated organization chart. Click a stage to edit its position and inspect current eligible employee names. The connectors add a stage; the inspector reorders or removes intermediate stages. Any configured position can be the final node, and nodes may be appended after Manager. Existing version/revision checks are retained.

For the current Uniform form, the administrator's chart is the default template. The requester can insert additional nodes and choose named employees before submission, or adjust unfinished nodes after submission. Required template nodes remain in order. Completed nodes, stored approvals and their remarks are immutable. Explicit assignments are tied to employee IDs; unassigned nodes continue to use the configured position. Deleted or duplicate people are rejected, and an employee cannot approve twice. Route edits are revision-checked, logged with before/after assignments and reflected in approval notifications.

Request progress keeps the existing node styling and runs vertically on desktop and mobile. Completed rows display the actual operator, Los Angeles processing time, action and remark. The detailed workflow log and form snapshot remain expandable. No production employee data or schedules are changed by this source update.
