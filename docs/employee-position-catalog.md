Employee positions use the title catalog in `public/public/employee-positions.js` for both Admin and Request workflow validation. The source is the supplied Organizational Chart 2026.xlsx: HQ 2026 and all 2026 station sheets (LAX, SFO, HNL, OGG/KOA/LIH, SAN, ONT, CBS/L&F/CC, DFW, AUS, IAH, EWR, ATL and JFK). Historical 2023/2024 sheets are excluded.

Only position titles are carried over. Names, station assignments, employee records, order, account links, and current approval workflows are not imported or automatically reassigned. Repeated titles are deduplicated; Senior/SR/SR SUP and spelling variants are written consistently. CSA is Agent, per the user's instruction. Existing Manager and Lead remain available so existing employee records and workflow snapshots keep working.

Administrators can select the added titles for employees and workflow stages. Workflow changes apply to new requests; existing requests retain their stages. The final stage supports the existing Manager title or Business Manager. Other titles do not automatically inherit approval authority: an employee must match the configured stage, and the same employee cannot approve twice. Read & Sign publishing permissions are unchanged.

The October 19 VISA syntax is already implemented in PR #1528: CHN GREEN CARD is Chinese residence, VS TO CAMBODIA binds the dated visa to Cambodia/KTI, and unqualified V EXP can use a checked Vietnam outbound route. The regression tests preserve destination, expiry and negative-evidence checks. No passenger names or passport numbers are included in new fixtures.

Approval Workflow now opens `/approval-workflow.html` as a dedicated organization chart. Click a stage to edit its position and inspect current eligible employee names. The connectors add a stage; the inspector reorders or removes intermediate stages. The final approval and existing version/revision checks are retained.
