    Start and end of time periods

    First week of each month starts with the 1st day of the month and ends with Thursday (end of payroll cycle) E.g. Mon to Thu

    (pre-populated)

    Following weeks start each Friday and end with Thursday (payroll cycles) E.g. Fri to Thu

    Last week of the month starts with Friday and ends with the last day of the month E.g. Fri to Mon

    Next month therefore would be: Tue 1Mar to Thu 3Mar

    Budgeted hours per flight (Net hrs) or Daily

    budgeted total labor hrs (given by Hosi) Always based on 1 flight. Weekly budget needs to be adjusted by the # of Actual flts + Rebilled Cancellations

    minus: allocated CBS hours (if applicable) (rebilled canx = full turn price)

    minus: allocated OPS hours (if applicable)

    plus: any additional hours for functions where we charge a regular flat fee.

    Budgeted hours wk (per week)

    Budgeted total labor automatically calculated ( Actual flights + rebillable cancellation X contracted hours daily)

    Do not add any hours which are regularly rebilled. Those hours will be credited again through the "rebill column" and will zero out the additional AE hours incurred.

    Hallmark Delays:

    only delays which are coded to Hallmark need to be indicated

    Length of Delays:

    only delays which are coded to Hallmark need to be indicated

    Rebillable Delays:

    refer to contract details: no grace period, 30min or 60min grace period to determine when a delay is rebillable

    Non-Rebillable Delays:

    refer to contract details: no grace period, 30min or 60min grace period to determine when a delay is rebillable

    Non-Rebillable Cancelations :

    refer to contract details: when Hallmark gets sufficient advance notice and not entire team is requested.

    If just additional labor is requested then it is a NON-rebillable delay, but additional hours are under rebill

    Rebillable Cancelations:

    refer to contract details: when Hallmark gets insufficient advance notice then full turn price is being charged (not under rebillable hours).

    Planned Flights:

    originally planned and scheduled flights

    Actual Flights:

    actual flights + cancellations (non-rebillable and billable canx) must match the number of planned flights.
